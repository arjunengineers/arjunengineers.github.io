Privacy

This website does not use cookies, analytics, tracking, or forms, and it does not collect or store any information about you.

What this site does

  • Every file, including the fonts, comes from this site’s own address. There are no third-party scripts, fonts, or embeds.
  • The project filter on the Work page keeps its state in the page address only. Nothing is stored or sent.
  • The color theme follows your device’s setting. If you choose Light or Dark instead, that choice is saved in your own browser so it is remembered on your next visit. It is never sent anywhere, and clearing your browser data removes it.
