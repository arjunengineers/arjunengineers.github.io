Outpatient Clinic Last-Mile Routing

Understanding the distance cost of delivery constraints.

Status
Academic optimization project
Context
Academic optimization project
My contribution
Routing-model development and comparison
Tools and methods
Gurobi, DFJ traveling-salesperson formulation, MTZ capacitated vehicle-routing formulation

A comparison of traveling-salesperson and capacity-constrained routing models for deliveries to eight outpatient clinics.

01The problem

A short route is not automatically an operationally feasible delivery plan. Serving eight outpatient clinics required considering vehicle capacity alongside travel distance.

02My approach

I developed and solved a DFJ traveling-salesperson model and an MTZ capacitated vehicle-routing model in Gurobi. The TSP supplied a distance benchmark, while the CVRP introduced the delivery-capacity constraints needed to examine a multi-vehicle plan.

03Reported model results

  • Eight outpatient clinics.
  • A three-van routing plan totaling 45.32 km under 35-box-unit vehicle-capacity constraints.
  • A 34.75 km TSP benchmark for comparison.

04Decision value

The comparison supported a discussion of fleet sizing and the tradeoff between distance efficiency and capacity feasibility. It helped distinguish a useful benchmark from an implementable routing structure.

Scope note

These are model results, not records of an operating delivery fleet. The route sequence, demand table, cost assumptions, and solver logs are not included in this portfolio.
